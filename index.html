About Kulikage Parts

Engineering-grade materials & processes

Kulikage Parts is an engineering brand by Nikolai Kulikov (Charlotte, NC). We produce sealing rings and custom parts with a focus on precise fit, service life, and efficiency. Materials include TPU 80A/95A, Viton, NBR, Nylon and composites. Orders by samples or drawings are welcome.

  • Individual fit & test assemblies
  • Materials tailored to oil/coolant/exhaust environments
  • Small batches with quick turnaround

Payment & Shipping: PayPal or Stripe invoice; local pickup in Charlotte. Worldwide shipping via USPS/UPS.

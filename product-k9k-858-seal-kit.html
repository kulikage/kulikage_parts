Why fix a boost leak

Leaking charge-air joints make the turbo work harder for the same boost and let metered air escape before it reaches the cylinders.

Typical symptoms
P0299 underboostSmoke under loadMore frequent DPF regenerationsHigher fuel consumptionOil mist around joints

New seals remove this cause and restore factory boost. P0299 can also come from the turbo, its actuator or the boost sensor — check the joints first; they're the cheapest fix.
